# ChaseAgent — WhatsApp Outreach Messages (human bait, ready to paste)
> Channel rules: WhatsApp = conversation, not broadcast. Hook first, link last, NEVER link as the first message in a group.
> Voice rules from VOICE.md still apply: no em-dashes, no AI words, short paragraphs, sounds like you typed it on your phone.

---

## THE 5 RULES (read before sending anything)
1. **Message 1 in any group = a question or story, no link, no pitch.** Get people talking first. Link only after 2+ people have replied to you.
2. **One message per group per week max.** You're a member, not a broadcaster.
3. **Reply to EVERY reply fast.** Conversation = trust = signups. The reply is where the selling happens, in DMs, one to one.
4. **DMs > groups.** The5 real business owners from PLAN.md get a personal message today. Personal beats viral here.
5. **Track it** in outreach/tracker.csv (sub column = "whatsapp").

## A. FOR BUSINESS OWNER GROUPS (trades, local business, service owners)

### A1. The poll question (message 1, no link)
> quick question for the group, what do you all do when a client goes past 30 days on an invoice? i've tried polite reminders, then firm ones, then just calling. curious if anyone has a system that actually works without making it weird

### A2. The story (message 1, no link, most human)
> had a client ghost me for 6 weeks on a 900 invoice. kept saying the check is in the mail. what finally worked was one short email with a hard small claims date, he paid in 2 days. kind of annoying that i had to threaten legal action just to get paid for work i already did

### A3. The value drop (message 2, after they reply, still no link)
> the thing that worked for me, write the 3 chase emails once and reuse them. day 3 is a soft nudge with the payment link. day 14 is firm, mentions the late fee from your terms. day 30 is a final notice with a real date on it. after that it's collections or small claims. the trick is the invoice is the bad guy, not you

### A4. The soft reveal (only after they ask "how do you do it" or "is there a tool")
> i got tired of writing those 3 emails by hand so i'm actually building a small tool for it, $5/mo, it drafts the chases and you tap approve before anything sends. it's early so i'm looking for 5 people to break it, here if you want to look: chaseagent.pages.dev

### A5. The blunt one (for when you've earned a bit of trust)
> i'm building chaseagent, a $5 tool that sends your day 3/14/30 invoice follow ups automatically, you approve every send. jobber charges $99 for a marketing suite that does less of this. if late payers are a monthly problem for you, take a look and tell me it's dumb: chaseagent.pages.dev

## B. FOR FOUNDER / BUILDING COMMUNITIES

### B1. The "roast my idea" opener (message 1, no link, founders love this)

- "i already use jobber/quickbooks" → this sits on top, it only does the chasing part. jobber does 40 things, this does 1 thing properly
- "is it live yet" → the site and waitlist are live, the product is in early access with the first 5 users. that's why i'm asking you
- "how do i know you're not going to spam my clients" → you approve every message. that's the whole point of the design
